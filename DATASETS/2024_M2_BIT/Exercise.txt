Electric Vehicle Charging Network (EVCN) 
GreenCharge Inc. is developing a smart charging network for electric vehicles across multiple cities. The system needs to manage charging stations and provide a seamless experience for EV owners. Here's the detailed description:
The company operates multiple charging stations across different locations. Each charging station contains several charging ports that can serve different types of electric vehicles. A charging station has a unique identifier, tracks its current status, and monitors its power capacity.
Users of the system must register with their personal information and can own multiple vehicles. Each vehicle has specific details including its model and compatible charger type. Users can register multiple payment methods to facilitate billing. For each payment method, users must provide the necessary details required to process payments (e.g., card number, expiration date, and security code for credit/debit cards or account details for bank transfers or e-wallets).
To use a charging port, users can make advance reservations for specific time slots. When a vehicle is being charged, the system creates a charging session that tracks the start time, end time, and energy consumed. Each charging session automatically generates a payment transaction which is linked to the chosen payment method of the EV owner. Such payment transaction should of course at least keep track of their status (paid/unpaid), amount and any discounts that might have applied.
The entire network of charging stations is monitored by load balancers that optimize power distribution across stations, especially during peak hours. Every load balancer is characterized by the load distribution algorithm it uses and some policy rules .Additionally, a maintenance operator keeps track of all charging stations and ports, monitoring their status and scheduling regular checks.
The system allows users flexibility in making multiple reservations, though it prevents scheduling conflicts by not permitting overlapping time slots. To ensure proper financial tracking, the system maintains a strict relationship between charging sessions and payments, where every charging session must generate exactly one corresponding payment transaction.
From a maintenance and monitoring perspective, the system implements comprehensive oversight mechanisms. A dedicated maintenance operator is responsible for monitoring all charging ports across a number of stations, ensuring their proper functioning and timely maintenance. Similarly, a load balancer plays a crucial role in managing the charging stations, optimizing power distribution and maintaining system stability.
The system also enforces several important operational rules.  On the user side, the system requires that users register at least one vehicle and one payment method before they can access the charging services. The charging ports are designed with flexibility in mind - they can either be empty or actively charging one vehicle. To maintain financial accountability, all payments in the system must be linked to a specific charging session, ensuring proper tracking and billing of charging activities.
